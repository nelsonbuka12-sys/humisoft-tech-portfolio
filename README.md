# HumiSoft Tech
### Software & Data Systems Design

**Practical Digital Solutions for Businesses and Organisations in Papua New Guinea**

HumiSoft Tech is an emerging software and IT services initiative focused on designing practical digital solutions that help businesses and organisations manage information, improve workflows, and operate more efficiently.

## Our Services

- **Software Development** — Custom business applications and management systems.
- **Database Design** — Structured databases, data management, and reporting solutions.
- **Website Development** — Professional websites and online information platforms.
- **Business Automation** — Digital forms, workflow improvements, and reporting tools.
- **Excel & Data Services** — Spreadsheet preparation, data organisation, and reports.
- **IT Support** — Technical assistance and troubleshooting.
